The HLSL helpers FurHash11, FurHash21 and FurHash22 in
Shared/Shaders/Fur/FurCommon.hlsl correspond to the hash11, hash12 and
hash22 algorithms, respectively. They retain the published arithmetic
and constants, with HLSL syntax and package-local names.
